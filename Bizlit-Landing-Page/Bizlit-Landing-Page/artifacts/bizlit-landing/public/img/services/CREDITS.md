## The hero comparison is drawn, not photographed

`img/services/room-furnished.svg` and `img/services/room-cleared.svg` are the same
illustrated living room — one furnished, one cleared and swept. Both are generated by
`scripts/make-room-pair.py` from a single room: the walls, boards, skirting, window and
its light are written once and painted into both frames, so the wipe lines up by
construction. No licence is needed for these two; they carry none of the credits below.
